# Naan & Curry Price Tracker

Live price comparison app for Restaurant Depot vs Sysco.

---

## Deploy to Vercel (one-time setup, ~10 minutes)

### Step 1 — Upload to GitHub

1. Go to **github.com** and sign in
2. Click the **+** button (top right) → **New repository**
3. Name it: `naan-curry-prices`
4. Set to **Private**
5. Click **Create repository**
6. On the next screen, click **uploading an existing file**
7. Drag and drop ALL the files and folders from this zip into the upload area
8. Click **Commit changes**

---

### Step 2 — Connect Vercel

1. Go to **vercel.com** and click **Sign Up**
2. Choose **Continue with GitHub** — sign in with your GitHub account
3. Click **Add New Project**
4. Find `naan-curry-prices` in the list → click **Import**
5. Leave all settings as default
6. Click **Deploy**
7. Wait ~60 seconds — Vercel builds and deploys the app
8. You'll see a URL like: `naan-curry-prices.vercel.app` — **that's your permanent link**

---

### Step 3 — Share with your team

Send the Vercel URL to your team. That's it. One link, forever.

---

## Adding to Home Screen (your team does this on their phone)

**iPhone (Safari only — must use Safari, not Chrome):**
1. Open the link in Safari
2. Tap the Share button (box with arrow pointing up)
3. Scroll down → tap **Add to Home Screen**
4. Tap **Add**
5. The app icon appears on their home screen — opens fullscreen like a real app

**Android (Chrome):**
1. Open the link in Chrome
2. Tap the three-dot menu (top right)
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**
4. Tap **Add**

---

## Updating the app in the future

When you make changes in Claude and get new code:

1. Go to your GitHub repo (`github.com/yourname/naan-curry-prices`)
2. Click on `src/App.jsx`
3. Click the **pencil icon** (Edit)
4. Select all, delete, paste the new code
5. Click **Commit changes**
6. Vercel automatically detects the change and redeploys in ~60 seconds
7. Same URL, same link — your team doesn't need to do anything

---

## Pricing data

All prices are stored in shared cloud storage — when anyone on your team scans or uploads a price, everyone sees it within 60 seconds. No per-device setup needed.
